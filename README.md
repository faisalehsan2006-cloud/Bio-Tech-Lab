
# Bio Tech Lab - Constraints

## Project Overview
This project identifies the major constraints that may affect the establishment of a Bio Tech Lab.

## Jira Project
Project Name: BIO TECH LAB

Epic:
Identify Constraints to be Imposed on the Solution for Bio Tech Lab

Epic ID: KAN-7

## 1. Economics Constraint

Economic constraints are related to the budget and financial resources required to establish a Bio Tech Lab.

The lab requires expensive equipment such as microscopes, centrifuges, incubators, and autoclaves. The university may have a limited budget, so essential equipment should be prioritized.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Total estimated budget is prepared.
- Equipment options are compared.
- Essential equipment is prioritized.

## 2. Politics Constraint

Political and regulatory constraints are related to government policies, university rules, and approvals.

The Bio Tech Lab may require permission from the university and relevant authorities. The lab must follow applicable biosafety rules and regulations.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Required university approvals are identified.
- Applicable biosafety rules are listed.
- Regulatory requirements are documented.

## 3. Technology Systems Constraint

Technology system constraints are related to equipment, computer systems, electricity, internet, and technical facilities.

The lab requires reliable electricity, computer systems for recording data, internet access, temperature control, and a power backup plan.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Power backup plan is prepared.
- Data recording system is identified.
- Internet and temperature control facilities are listed.
- Necessary technical equipment is identified.
